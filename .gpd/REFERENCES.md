## Quantum Field Theory in de Sitter / Curved Spacetime

### Bunch & Davies (1978) — Bunch-Davies Vacuum
T. S. Bunch and P. C. W. Davies, "Quantum field theory in de Sitter space: renormalization by point-splitting," *Proc. R. Soc. Lond.* A **360**, 117–134 (1978).
DOI: 10.1098/rspa.1978.0060
*Primary reference for the Bunch-Davies vacuum state and Wightman function in de Sitter.*

### Allen (1985) — Vacuum States in de Sitter Space
B. Allen, "Vacuum states in de Sitter space," *Phys. Rev. D* **32**, 3136–3149 (1985).
DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevD.32.3136
*Derives Wightman function for full one-parameter family of de Sitter-invariant states; standard reference for VALD-01.*

### Gibbons & Hawking (1977) — Gibbons-Hawking Temperature
G. W. Gibbons and S. W. Hawking, "Cosmological event horizons, thermodynamics, and particle creation," *Phys. Rev. D* **15**, 2738–2751 (1977).
DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevD.15.2738
*Establishes T_GH = H/(2π) for cosmological horizons; anchor for VALD-03.*
